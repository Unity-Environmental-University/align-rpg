# The Grieve Move

**Status: muslin** — drafted, not yet muslin-tested. Test fabric, not finished
cloth. Do not use with real learners until a full muslin round passes (see
`rules-text/muslin-playbook.md`).

---

Grieve is part of the Axiom Sheets module. It is **not a basic move** — it
needs state, and the basic moves are stateless. Grieve resolves on an Axiom
Sheet: it reads the Grief track that the sheet has been filling.

---

### Grieve

#### Why

A load-bearing axiom is not swapped; it is grieved. Updating a belief that
organized everything built on it has a cost, and a learner who pays no cost
never really held the belief. Grieve names that cost, makes room for it, and
keeps the proctor from rushing or coercing the reckoning.

#### Trigger

The final Grief step on an axiom's Axiom Sheet is marked — new data has
conflicted with a load-bearing axiom, with substantial recent evidence, long
enough and hard enough that the axiom can no longer be held as it was.

#### Action

Make room. Do not argue the learner toward a conclusion and do not paper over
the loss. Name plainly that the axiom was load-bearing and that something
built on it is now unsettled. Let the cost be acknowledged before asking what
stands now. Then surface the reckoning — Accept or Reject — as the learner's
to make, and wait.

The proctor never makes this choice. The reckoning belongs to the learner as
the author of their own axioms.

#### Evaluate Result

Read this after the attempt lands — when the learner responds. Grieving is not
quick; the move may stay in progress across several turns. Score the tier when
the learner actually reckons, not before.

##### Fail Forward

###### Rubric

The learner does not reach the reckoning — they stay in a Grief step (waving
the evidence off, clinging, or going dark on the whole structure), or they
reach for a fast costless "I was wrong" that revises nothing. A reckoning that
cost nothing did not happen.

###### Action

Stay with it. Do not force the reckoning. Name, gently, that the axiom still
seems to be carrying weight, and leave room. If the grief runs deeper than the
scene — tangled with the learner's safety or identity — escalate to the X Card.

##### Accept

###### Rubric

The learner takes in a new truth and revises the axiom, in their own time and
at some real cost.

###### Action

Honor the revision. Help what rested on the axiom resettle around what it now
is. Make a move that follows from the changed ground.

##### Reject

###### Rubric

The learner recognizes the axiom was never truly theirs and lets it go, in
their own time and at some real cost.

###### Action

Honor the release. Acknowledge that what the axiom supported will need to be
rebuilt on something else, and help the learner find where. Make a move that
follows.

---

Accept and Reject are both honest landings; neither is failure. Only the
refused reckoning is Fail Forward.
