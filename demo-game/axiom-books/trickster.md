# The Trickster  (axiom book)

**Status: muslin** — drafted, not yet muslin-tested.

## One who moves through gaps. The Trickster's strength is not deception; it is
the refusal to accept that the rules in front of them are the only rules.

---

### Axiom: "Every rule has a seam."

Built upon: the Trickster's eye for the unguarded route; their faith that no
system is closed; their impatience with "that is just how it is."

```
Stress  O O O O O
Grief   Denial[ ] Anxiety[ ] Depression[ ]
```

  **Move — Find the Seam**
  Trigger: the Trickster faces a rule, wall, or system that is presented as
  total.
  Action: look for the gap the system did not mean to leave, and name it.
  Evaluate Result (deferred — read on the response): Fail Forward (there is no
  seam here, or the seam is a trap) / Partial Success (you find a seam, but
  going through it costs or reveals something) / Full Success (the way through
  opens cleanly).

*If this axiom is Released, the Trickster can no longer Find the Seam — they
have come to believe some walls are genuinely solid.*

---

### Axiom: "I owe nothing I did not agree to."

Built upon: the Trickster's wariness of inherited debt; their habit of slipping
obligations; their hard-won autonomy.

```
Stress  O O O O O
Grief   Denial[ ] Anxiety[ ] Depression[ ]
```

  **Move — Slip the Knot**
  Trigger: an obligation is being placed on the Trickster that they did not
  choose.
  Action: refuse the obligation, or transform it into one you would choose.
  Evaluate Result (deferred): Fail Forward (the obligation holds, or slipping
  it costs someone who did not deserve the cost) / Partial Success (you slip
  it, but it lands on the scene some other way) / Full Success (you are free,
  cleanly).

*This axiom is a likely Grieve candidate. Pressed by evidence of obligations
worth keeping — to friends, to community — it strains. Revised (toward "I owe
what I choose to owe, and some bonds are worth choosing") it keeps Slip the
Knot but changes what counts as an unchosen knot.*

---

### Axiom: "If I am not the cleverest in the room, I am not safe."

Built upon: the Trickster's compulsion to have the last move; their difficulty
being helped; their loneliness.

```
Stress  O O O O O
Grief   Denial[ ] Anxiety[ ] Depression[ ]
```

*This axiom carries no move. It grants nothing — it only shapes how the
Trickster plays, and it is the one the game most wants them to Grieve. A
Trickster who never grieves this cannot be met.*
