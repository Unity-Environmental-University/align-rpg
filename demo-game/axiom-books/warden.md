# The Warden  (axiom book)

**Status: muslin** — drafted, not yet muslin-tested.

## One who stands between others and harm. The Warden's strength is not force;
it is the refusal to step aside.

---

### Axiom: "The vulnerable are mine to shield."

Built upon: the Warden's habit of putting their own body in the gap; the trust
others place in them; their sense of why they are here at all.

```
Stress  O O O O O
Grief   Denial[ ] Anxiety[ ] Depression[ ]
```

  **Move — Stand Between**
  Trigger: harm is moving toward someone the Warden could shield.
  Action: put yourself in the gap — physically, socially, or in the fiction —
  and take what was meant for them.
  Evaluate Result (deferred — read on the response): Fail Forward (the harm
  reaches them anyway, or your shielding takes something from them they did
  not want taken) / Partial Success (you take the harm, but at a cost you did
  not choose) / Full Success (the harm stops with you, and they are free to
  act).

*If this axiom is Released, the Warden can no longer Stand Between — they have
stopped believing the gap is theirs to fill.*

---

### Axiom: "I can carry more than I am asked to."

Built upon: the Warden's reluctance to hand off weight; their slowness to rest;
their quiet pride in endurance.

```
Stress  O O O O O
Grief   Denial[ ] Anxiety[ ] Depression[ ]
```

  **Move — Bear the Weight**
  Trigger: a burden in the scene is too much for those present to carry.
  Action: take the burden onto yourself so the scene can continue.
  Evaluate Result (deferred): Fail Forward (the weight is genuinely too much;
  something gives) / Partial Success (you carry it, but you cannot also do the
  thing you were doing) / Full Success (you carry it, and the way forward
  opens).

*This axiom is a likely Grieve candidate: "I can carry more than I am asked
to" meets the evidence of a Warden's own limits. Revising it (to something
like "I can carry what is mine, and ask for the rest") keeps Bear the Weight
but changes when it fires.*

---

### Axiom: "Standing firm is the same as being right."

Built upon: the Warden's difficulty changing course; their read of compromise
as failure.

```
Stress  O O O O O

```

*This axiom carries no move. It grants no action — it only shapes how the
Warden plays, and it is the axiom the game most wants them to eventually
Grieve. A Warden who never grieves this one cannot bend.*
