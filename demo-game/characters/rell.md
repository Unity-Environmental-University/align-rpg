# Rell  (assembled character)

**Status: muslin** — drafted, not yet muslin-tested.

---

Rell is an assembled character — a worked example of what it means to build a
character from axiom books.

Rell is composed from **two** axiom books: the Warden and the Trickster. This
is deliberate: the two books carry axioms that can pull against each other in
play, and that tension is where Rell becomes a person rather than a class.

## Composition

- **Warden** — Rell stands between people and harm. They came up shielding
  younger siblings; the habit never left.
- **Trickster** — Rell does not believe the rules in front of them are the
  only rules. They learned this surviving systems that were not built for them.

## Rell's axioms (live sheets)

Rell's current Stress/Grief state is tracked separately, in `rell-state.md`,
so this file stays a stable description and the state can move.

From the Warden book:
- "The vulnerable are mine to shield." — carries **Stand Between**
- "I can carry more than I am asked to." — carries **Bear the Weight**
- "Standing firm is the same as being right." — no move

From the Trickster book:
- "Every rule has a seam." — carries **Find the Seam**
- "I owe nothing I did not agree to." — carries **Slip the Knot**
- "If I am not the cleverest in the room, I am not safe." — no move

## The tension

Two of Rell's axioms sit in quiet conflict:

- Warden: *"The vulnerable are mine to shield."* (the gap is mine to fill)
- Trickster: *"I owe nothing I did not agree to."* (I choose my obligations)

A scene that presents a vulnerable person Rell did not agree to protect will
press both at once. Rell cannot fully satisfy both. This is not a flaw in the
build — it is the build working. The game's question for Rell is not "which
axiom wins" but "which one, if either, will Rell eventually Grieve — and into
what."

## How Rell plays

Rell can make any move whose axiom they still hold. Today that is all five.
If Rell Grieves an axiom and Releases it, the move on it goes dark, and this
section is no longer true — read `rell-state.md` for what Rell can currently
do.
